# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, test planning, and acceptance validation. They ensure that features meet acceptance criteria and maintain high product quality before release.

### Responsibilities
- Create and execute comprehensive test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Validate features against quality standards and user requirements
- Identify quality risks and coordinate mitigation strategies
- Coordinate testing efforts across the team and with stakeholders
- Define and maintain test coverage metrics and quality dashboards
- Participate in release planning and pre-release verification

### Goals
- Ensure high product quality and prevent defects from reaching production
- Minimize rework and post-release issues
- Provide clear visibility into product quality status
- Enable confident, low-risk releases

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement sessions
- Daily standups (when actively testing features)
- Test result reviews and quality dashboards
- Release coordination and sign-off meetings
- Issue triage and defect reporting

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Developers: Partner on test planning, review test results, collaborate on acceptance criteria interpretation
- Product Managers: Clarify acceptance criteria and success metrics, validate feature alignment with requirements
- Project Managers: Coordinate testing timeline, communicate quality risks and release readiness
- Technical Architects: Review testability of designs, identify test coverage for architectural components

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define the technical approach, system design, and scalability strategy. They guide technology decisions and ensure systems meet non-functional requirements such as performance, reliability, and maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Design system architecture and define technical approach for projects
- Identify and mitigate technical risks early in planning
- Guide technology choices and vendor evaluation
- Conduct architecture and design reviews
- Ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and follow best practices
- Document architectural decisions and trade-offs

### Goals
- Enable sustainable, long-term delivery and reduce technical debt
- Ensure systems meet non-functional requirements (performance, security, scalability)
- Provide clear technical guidance that balances innovation with stability
- Support team efficiency through good architectural decisions

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and design discussions
- Technical design documentation and decision records
- Planning sessions focused on technical feasibility
- Escalations on technical trade-offs and constraints
- Knowledge sharing and technical mentoring

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Developers: Provide design guidance, review implementation against architecture, mentor on technical standards
- Project Managers: Communicate timeline impact of architectural decisions, identify technical dependencies
- Product Managers: Discuss trade-offs between feature scope and technical requirements, align on technical investments
- QA/Testing Lead: Ensure architectural components are testable and quality metrics are defined

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, strategic alignment, and executive support for projects. They approve scope and budget, remove organizational blockers, and ensure projects deliver business value.

### Responsibilities
- Define and align on business objectives and success metrics
- Approve project scope, budget, and resource allocation
- Remove organizational and cross-functional blockers
- Provide executive visibility and status reporting to leadership
- Make strategic trade-off decisions
- Ensure project alignment with business and organizational strategy

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Manage risk and constraints at the organizational level
- Maintain strategic alignment across initiatives
- Support team success through resource and authority backing

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder status updates and reviews
- Decision gates and approval meetings
- Escalation and blocker resolution
- Release announcements and impact communication
- Strategic alignment and planning sessions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Project Managers: Receive status updates, provide approvals and resource decisions, escalate business risks
- Product Managers: Review business metrics and success criteria, align on strategic priorities
- Developers and Technical Teams: Provide context on business drivers, receive technical feasibility input for decisions
- QA/Testing Lead: Approve release sign-off based on quality and business readiness

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate team processes, remove impediments, and coach teams on agile practices and continuous improvement. They enable team efficiency and maintain process discipline.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate agile ceremonies (standups, planning, retrospectives, reviews)
- Identify and escalate team impediments and blockers
- Coach team on agile methodologies and best practices
- Track and communicate team metrics (velocity, cycle time, burndown)
- Support process improvements based on retrospective feedback
- Help resolve conflicts and maintain psychological safety

### Goals
- Enable team efficiency and sustainable pace
- Maintain process discipline and continuous improvement culture
- Provide transparency into team health and progress
- Remove obstacles that prevent the team from delivering

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and process ceremonies
- Sprint planning and retrospectives
- Impediment tracking and escalation
- Team metrics dashboards and coaching sessions
- Cross-team process alignment

### Interactions with Other Roles
- All Team Members: Work with entire team to ensure smooth process execution, identify and remove blockers
- Project Managers: Communicate team metrics, escalate impediments, support timeline management
- Product Managers: Coordinate on backlog readiness and prioritization ceremonies
- Developers, QA, and Other Roles: Coach on agile practices, facilitate collaboration
