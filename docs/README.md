# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository contains the core process documentation used by OctoAcme to plan, deliver, release, and improve project work. The docs provide a consistent project-management model for cross-functional teams and act as a single starting point for understanding how initiatives move from idea to delivery and continuous improvement.

## Project management processes summary

OctoAcme uses a customer-first, iterative lifecycle with clear ownership and evidence-based decision making. The process starts with **initiation**, when the team validates the business need, defines the goal and success metrics, identifies stakeholders, highlights initial risks, and decides whether to move forward. Once the project is approved, the team enters **planning**, where the work is translated into a prioritized backlog, acceptance criteria, estimates, dependencies, milestones, and a release plan.

During **execution and tracking**, work is managed through a project board with stages such as Backlog, Ready, In Progress, In Review, QA, and Done. The team emphasizes small, reviewable pull requests, visible status reporting, and clear communication around blockers and dependencies. The project docs also define specific roles and responsibilities so that planning, execution, and approval remain clear across functions.

**Communication** is central to OctoAcme's delivery model. Teams hold daily standups to surface progress and blockers, weekly syncs to review status and risks, and milestone or stakeholder updates to maintain alignment. Risks and dependencies are tracked in a risk register, and escalation follows a clear path from the delivery team to the Project Manager, Product Lead, and Sponsor as needed. This keeps the team responsive while preserving accountability and transparency.

Quality assurance is woven into each phase. New logic should include unit tests, relevant integration tests, and smoke testing for critical workflows. CI should cover linting, automated tests, and security checks, and release readiness depends on acceptance criteria, rollback planning, deployment verification, and stakeholder communication. After each sprint, release, or significant milestone, the team should conduct a retrospective to capture what went well, what needs improvement, and what action items should be tracked going forward.

## Roles and personas

OctoAcme's process is built around the following roles:

- Project Managers coordinate delivery schedules, risks, dependencies, and communication.
- Product Managers define outcomes, backlog priority, and success criteria.
- Developers implement features, contribute to design and reviews, and maintain code quality.
- QA/Testing validates acceptance criteria, release readiness, and overall quality.
- Stakeholders contribute business context, approvals, and strategic alignment.
